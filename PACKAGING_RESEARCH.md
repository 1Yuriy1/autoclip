# Subject-first video packaging

Research checked September 8, 2026. Applied in AutoClip upload kits.

## Observations and evidence

- Rockstar Games' [Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QdBZY2fkU-0) showed approximately 288 million views in the retrieved index. Its franchise is immediately recognizable. An official launch, distribution and audience demand are major confounders; its view count does not prove that wording caused its success.
- MrBeast's [$456,000 Squid Game In Real Life!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0e3GPea1Tyg) showed approximately 943 million indexed views. It combines a known property with a specific premise and tangible stakes. AutoClip should borrow specificity, not invent prize amounts or promises.
- YouTube's [thumbnail and title tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en) recommend accuracy, concise titles, important words near the beginning, readable text and uncomplicated imagery. Searchable and intriguing titles serve different audiences.
- YouTube's [description guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en) recommends carrying the main subject into the first lines of a unique description.
- YouTube explains that [thumbnail experiments use watch time](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-june-14-2024/), incorporating both clicks and viewing. [Title and thumbnail comparison](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/) supports testing alternatives. AutoClip's suggestions are candidate pairs, not completed A/B tests or predicted winners.

## Implemented behavior

The project provides an editable main subject. GTA 6 and Grand Theft Auto VI are normalized to GTA 6; other projects use a conservative source-title prefix. Upload kits put that subject first, remove editing suffixes such as “1-minute cut,” supply alternative pairs, and carry the subject into the description and thumbnail badge. A thumbnail's short hook complements its title.

For the supplied task-force scene, transcript evidence supports a pickup, the approaching task force and a situation. The recommended pair is “GTA 6: A Simple Pickup Turns Into a Raid” with “THEY’RE HERE.” Rules only use that angle when the relevant dialogue is present. Sparse or unrelated content falls back to the existing clip topic; it does not invent raids, secrets, view statistics or outcomes.

This is deterministic metadata/transcript-based packaging, not a new live competitor scraper or verified visual content classifier. Existing saved edits are preserved. Refresh suggestions deliberately creates a new editable draft. Assess published results using the channel's own impressions, CTR, watch time and retention.
