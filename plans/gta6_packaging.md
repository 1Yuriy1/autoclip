## 3. Credit line

Every clip gets a credit line in the caption (and in the YouTube description or pinned comment, if the platform allows).

**Format:**
`Clip from {streamer} ({platform} @{handle}) · full stream: {source_url} · {stream_date}`

**Campaign variant (add the rules text the campaign requires):**
`Credit: {streamer} @{handle} · Source: {source_url} · {RULES_NOTE}`

**Rules:**
- `{source_url}` is the original VOD or channel link. It is required.
- `{handle}` must match the streamer's account on the platform that the clip is posted to, if they have one. If they don't, use the channel name shown on the stream.
- Do not add a credit to a streamer who has not been cleared under the rights rules. Drop the clip instead.
- Yuriy's own gameplay: `Gameplay: Yuriy ({own handle})`. Keep the credit line for the proof trail anyway.

- TikTok ET: Mon 7am/10am/10pm · Tue 2am/4am/9am · Wed 7am/8am/11pm · Thu 9am/12pm/7pm · Fri 5am/1pm/3pm · Sat 11am/7pm/8pm · Sun 7am/8am/4pm
- YouTube Shorts ET: Mon–Wed 2pm/4pm/6pm · Thu–Fri 12pm/3pm/6pm · Sat–Sun 9am/12pm/3pm

### Source 2: Buffer, "Best time to post 2026" (2026 State of Social Media Engagement, 52M+ posts)
Source: https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-social-media (fetched 2026-11-12)
- TikTok (7.1M posts): best Sunday 9am; Monday 1pm and Sunday 1pm next; evenings 6–11pm daily; Saturday best day overall.
- YouTube Shorts: best Friday 4pm; Friday 6pm and 7pm strong; weekday evenings 6–11pm.
- Instagram (~10M posts): best Thursday 9am; weekdays 9am and 6pm; Wednesday highest engagement by day.
- Timezone caveat: Buffer says its times "apply to any local time zone," so the ET conversion is my assumption. Treat the Buffer clock times as approximate in ET.

### Source 3: Adobe Express, Instagram Reels (rejected for the schedule)
Source: https://ppc.land/instagram-reels-posted-at-3-a-m-gain-2-1-times-the-views-of-9-p-m-posts/ (search snippet only; full study not fetched). Reels posted at 3am ET averaged 22.2M views against 10.6M at 9pm ET, from 20,247 Reels by 149 creators. I did not use this for the schedule: one sample, creators not gaming-specific, and the 3am figure contradicts the other sources. Use only as a test idea.
