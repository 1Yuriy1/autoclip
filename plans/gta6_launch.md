# GTA 6 Launch Clipping Plan
Owner: Showrunner · Created 2026-11-12 · Launch: Thu 2026-11-19 (midnight ET; PS5 + Xbox Series X|S, no PC)

## Goal
Clip GTA 6 launch-window content from popular streamers where the traffic is, on TikTok / YouTube Shorts / IG Reels, and get paid via clipping campaigns (Whop Content Rewards etc.) or own-page growth.

## Rights rules (non-negotiable)
- Only clip a streamer if: (a) a paid campaign covers them, or (b) they publicly say "clip me" / allow clipping, with a saved proof link or screenshot. Otherwise drop.
- Yuriy's own gameplay is always OK.
- NO pre-release material (Rockstar policy bars monetizing pre-release content): no leaks, no early-copy footage, no rebroadcast of the Netflix "Extended Look".
- Mute/replace in-game radio music (Rockstar bars monetizing its music; also Content ID risk).
- YouTube: no heavy gore/dismemberment in first 7 seconds (loses ad monetization).
- Nothing gets posted or submitted without Yuriy's OK per clip.

## Timeline
- Nov 12–18 (prep): Scout builds source list + campaign list with proof. Cutter tests AutoClip on long Twitch/Kick VODs. Editor builds facecam+gameplay vertical template. Packager builds title/hashtag bank.
- Nov 18 night – Nov 19: launch streams go live. Cut from VODs within hours; first-mover window is ~48–72h.
- Nov 19–Dec 3: daily batches. Target 10–20 approved clips/day across streamers.

## What wins (clip types)
1. Streamer first-reaction moments (opening, map reveal, first heist)
2. Fails/chaos/police chases with strong reaction
3. "Things you didn't know" / hidden details (works with own gameplay)
4. Rage/funny chat moments

## Open items for Yuriy
- Whop account sign-in (to join campaigns once Scout confirms one)
- Posting accounts: dedicated GTA page(s) on TikTok / YT / IG?
- Will he play GTA 6 himself (own footage = zero rights risk)?
